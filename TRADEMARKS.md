# Name and logo policy

The code of couchPie is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License (see
[LICENSE](LICENSE)). The license covers the code; it does not give rights to the couchPie
name or logo. This policy says how they may be used, so that "couchPie" always means the
official project, and people know what they are getting.

The couchPie name and logo belong to the project's maintainer (see
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).

## You may, without asking

- Refer to the project by name: in articles, videos, talks, tutorials, reviews and
  discussions.
- Say that your software or hardware "works with couchPie", or that your fork "is based on
  couchPie", as long as it is clear that it isn't the official project.
- Use the name for non-commercial community activities about the project, such as a user
  group, a meetup or a workshop.
- Share unmodified copies of the official couchPie software, under its name, free of charge.

## Please ask first

- Selling hardware, kits or services under the couchPie name, or with the name or logo on the
  product or its packaging.
- Using the logo on merchandise, or in a modified form.

Write to the maintainer through an issue on the official repository.

## Not allowed

- Naming a fork, a product, an app or a domain "couchPie" or something that could be confused
  with it.
- Distributing a modified version of couchPie under the couchPie name.
- Suggesting that the project or its maintainer endorses, supports or is a partner of you or
  your product when it isn't.

## Forks

Forks are welcome; that is part of free software. A fork that is distributed needs its own
name and logo. It may say it is "based on couchPie" with a link to the official repository.

## Other companies' names

Netflix, Prime Video, HBO Max, Disney+, Paramount+, Globoplay, YouTube, Steam, Raspberry Pi,
Samsung and the other names of services and products mentioned in this project are trademarks
of their owners. couchPie is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Their logos are not
part of the repository; each user supplies the logos for their own home screen.
