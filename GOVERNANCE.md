# Governance

## What couchPie is for

couchPie aims to be:

- **a saviour for old smart TVs and a media centre**: a home screen, the streaming services,
  your own media and your home, driven by the TV's own remote and your phone;
- **a low-cost game platform**: retro games, games streamed from a PC, and phones as
  controllers, so that playing doesn't require buying a console;
- **a party games enabler**: games for the living room, played by friends with their phones.

It is meant to be cheap and free to use, especially where consoles and games are expensive,
as in Brazil, and to stay open for anyone who wants to learn from it, change it or improve it.

## Who decides

couchPie is led by its founder and maintainer, Gabriel Loureiro Figueira (GitHub:
Gabrielloufi), who has the final say on:

- the project's direction and roadmap;
- what is merged into the official repository and released;
- the project's name, logo and visual identity (see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)).

Decisions are made in the open where possible: in issues and pull requests, with reasons.
As the project grows, the maintainer may invite trusted contributors to review and merge
changes in parts of the project; this document will list them.

## Proposing changes

Open an issue describing the idea before starting large work (see
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). A proposal that doesn't fit the project's direction may
be declined even when it is good work; you are free to build it in a fork.

## Open source, and a box for convenience

- couchPie's software is and stays free and open source, under `AGPL-3.0-or-later`. Anyone may
  use it, change it, and share it, under that license.
- The maintainer may in the future sell a ready-made couchPie box. The box is about
  convenience (assembled, set up and supported), not about features: the software in it is the
  same open software, with its source available.
- Contributions are accepted under the project's license with a sign-off (the DCO), not under
  a contributor license agreement. Contributors keep the copyright to their work, and their
  contributions stay under the AGPL.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the license. A fork that is distributed must use a different name,
as described in [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md), and may say that it is based on couchPie.

## Changes to this document

The maintainer may change this document; changes are made in the repository, where their
history is visible.
